Biography

I have lived in Florida my whole life. Growing up so close to the space coast has inspired in me a love for all things related to flight and science fiction. This led me to join Florida Polytechnic University to pursue a bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering. I have a particular interest in the aerospace industry. I enjoy working hard to improve my skills in all areas of science and technology, and have experience with simulation, CAD, coding, and design. Some of my hobbies outside of school include amateur rocketry, radio, board games, and raising livestock.
